How it works

  • Time prediction follows Survival-function-analysys.ipynb: a model gives the survival function S(t) of the patient, and the predicted time is RMST(τ), the area under S(t) up to τ = 1500 days. It is compared with min(time, τ) from the dataset.
  • Models (CoxPH, Weibull AFT, XGBoost Cox, NN and reservoir network with Cox calibration) are trained exactly as in the notebook: the same 326 training patients (stratified split, random_state=37), StandardScaler and hyperparameters. C-index and Brier score are measured on the 82 held-out patients.
  • t-SNE is computed in the browser on the 30 standardized parameters (exact method, PCA initialization, 1000 iterations, as in scikit-learn). When you edit a patient, it is placed onto the fixed map by out-of-sample t-SNE: only that point moves, from its dataset position (hollow circle) to the new one.
  • The same prediction from the command line:
python scripts/survival_time_predictor.py predict --id 1444 --set age=70 llo=1
